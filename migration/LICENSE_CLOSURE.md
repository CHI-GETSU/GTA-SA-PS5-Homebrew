## Other identified components

- **DXVK:** all ten DLLs were matched to the official 2.6.2-prospero2 artifact; exact source a6f6dcc1 and zlib/libpng notice are retained. Its build submodules remain to be fetched at the upstream gitlink revisions for a full developer rebuild. This is not treated as a blanket GPL corresponding-source hold on permissively licensed DXVK.
- **Mesa WGL/Zink:** separate PS5_Mesa e4607ef6 source pin/recipe and shipped manifest exist. Verify its matching changes, retained component notices and actual linked platform code; do not conflate it with the RADV or launcher Mesa versions.
- **Fonts, artwork, FreeType, LLVM/MinGW, certificate/PSL data:** notices/provenance must remain in the full package. Credits do not replace licenses. Do not introduce commercial GTA or proprietary Sony SDK/firmware as a way to fill a dependency gap.
- **Metadata:** the disclosed build username paths and prefix host-name setting remain a separate owner decision. They are not API secrets and are not reasons to rebuild the entire runtime. Title-ID migration does not resolve that privacy decision.

## Concrete completion evidence

A releasable candidate needs an artifact-to-source manifest covering the actual linked components, accessible complete source/patch sets and required build/relink materials, retained notices, and an explicit resolution of the applicable GPL/LGPL distribution conditions. Source completeness must be demonstrated; a collection of version labels, license texts or successful host axis tests is not that demonstration. Work can first complete the source bundle and scripts while keeping the validated binaries untouched.
